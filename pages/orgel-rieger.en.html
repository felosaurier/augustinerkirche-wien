The Rieger Organ

The great organ of the Augustinian Church: built in 1976 in the historic case of 1785, with 48 stops on four manuals and pedal.

High Masses The Reil Organ
  • 1976Built by Rieger Orgelbau, Schwarzach (Vorarlberg)
  • 4Manuals and pedal
  • 48Stops
  • 5000Combinations

The great organ was built in 1976 by the organ builders Rieger (Schwarzach, Vorarlberg). The historic case by Johann Hencke from 1785 was preserved.

Until the Second World War it housed an instrument that Johann Hencke had built for the Church of the Black Spaniards and that was installed at St. Augustine at the end of the 18th century. The group of angels on the case is by the Rococo sculptor Johann Baptist Straub.

The instrument has 48 stops on four manuals and pedal, with slider chests, mechanical key action and electric stop action. In 2002 the organ builders Eisenbarth (Passau) fitted it with a modern combination system during a general overhaul. In 2018 Rieger Orgelbau cleaned the organ, and in 2019 the company renewed the combination system. In 2023 a 32′ Untersatz, a Zimbelstern and a glockenspiel were added.

Its sound is equally at home with music of the Renaissance, the Baroque, the Romantic era and the 20th century up to Olivier Messiaen. In 2026 the organ celebrates its fiftieth anniversary.
