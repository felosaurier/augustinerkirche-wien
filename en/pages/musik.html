Direction of Church Music

The Director of Church Music and the parish priest jointly lead the church music of St. Augustine. Together they ensure that the centuries-old tradition of Musica sacra resounds in the Augustinian Church every Sunday, to the glory of God and the joy of people.

KMD MMag. Peter Tiefengraber, MA
Director of Church Music

Peter Tiefengraber was born in 1991 and grew up in Pinkafeld (Burgenland). After lessons with his uncle (piano) and at the local music school (organ), he was enrolled from 2005 as an extraordinary student at the University of Music and Performing Arts Graz, Oberschützen Institute. From 2010 he studied organ and instrumental pedagogy with Ulrike Theresia Wegele at the Joseph Haydn Conservatory in Eisenstadt. From 2011 he went on to study music theory (with Gesine Schröder) and later continued his organ studies (with Pier Damiano Peretti; diploma examination in 2017 with distinction) at the University of Music and Performing Arts Vienna. Since 2017 he has been studying Catholic church music. Organ masterclasses with Michael Radulescu, Ludger Lohmann, Jon Laukvik, Jürgen Essl, Luigi Ferdinando Tagliavini, Jean-Claude Zehnder and Michel Bouvard were artistically inspiring.

He is in demand both as an organist and as a music theorist at home and abroad and has published articles on music theory, including on Johann Nepomuk David’s Gottesminnelieder and on Anton Bruckner’s metrical numbers. As a performer he has appeared in Germany, France, Austria and Scotland as well as in televised and broadcast Masses.

Since 2015 he has been heard regularly as a guest organist at the Augustinian Church in Vienna and at Eisenstadt Cathedral. With the choir of the Pinkafeld parish he performs great orchestral Masses by Mozart and Haydn every year. In September 2021 he took over the overall direction of the church music of St. Augustine as Director of Church Music.

Father Mag. Matthias Schlögl OSA
Parish priest of St. Augustine

Father Matthias has been parish priest of the Augustinian Church since 2011 and Dean of the City Deanery Vienna 1 since 2024. From February 2008 to August 2021 he was responsible for the planning, organisation and financing of the church music of St. Augustine.

Together with his confreres in the Augustinian monastery and HR Dr. Margarete Strassnig-Bachner he founded the Sponsorship of St. Augustine and won Franz Welser-Möst as patron.

As parish priest he ensures that liturgy and church music form a unity. This shapes the solemn High Masses on Sundays and holy days, which are known far beyond Vienna and are celebrated by hundreds of the faithful from near and far. More about him under The Augustinians in Vienna (in German).

Choir of St. Augustine

In every service God draws near to his people, and the congregation responds with its praise. The Choir of St. Augustine helps the worshipping congregation to lift up their hearts to God and sings the praise of the Creator on their behalf, in classical as well as contemporary works.

Like the Church itself, a church choir is unity in diversity, praising God with one voice. New singers are warmly welcome.

Orchestra of St. Augustine

The orchestra is an essential part of the church music of St. Augustine. Most of its players are freelance professional musicians who are also permanent members of ensembles such as the Wiener Akademie (Martin Haselböck) or the Concilium musicum (Christoph Angerer). They are familiar with historical performance practice, which benefits the mainly classical and early Romantic repertoire of the High Masses.

Members of the great Viennese orchestras also play regularly, for example from the Vienna Philharmonic, the State Opera, the Volksoper and the Vienna Radio Symphony Orchestra. The Director of Church Music is responsible for the line-up.

The Organs of St. Augustine

The Augustinian Church has two organs: the great Rieger organ of 1976 and the small Reil organ of 1985, the “Vienna Bach Organ”.

The Rieger Organ

Built
1976 by Rieger Orgelbau, Schwarzach (Vorarlberg)
Case
Johann Hencke, 1785
Size
4 manuals and pedal, 48 stops
Renewed
2002 Eisenbarth (Passau), 2018 cleaning and 2019 new combination system by Rieger, 2023 32′ Untersatz, Zimbelstern and glockenspiel

The great organ was newly built in 1976 by the organ builders Rieger. The historic case by Johann Hencke from 1785 was preserved. Until the Second World War it housed an instrument that Johann Hencke had built for the Church of the Black Spaniards and that was installed at St. Augustine at the end of the 18th century. The group of angels on the case is by the Rococo sculptor Johann Baptist Straub.

All about the Rieger organ

The Reil Organ

Built
1985 by the Reil brothers, Heerde (Netherlands)
Size
2 manuals and pedal, 25 stops
Design
historical, after Gottfried Silbermann and Tobias Trost; slider chests, mechanical action, Werckmeister III temperament
Owner
Parish of St. Augustine

The small organ was commissioned by the Vienna Festival for the anniversary year “300 years of Johann Sebastian Bach” and consecrated by Cardinal Franz König, then Archbishop of Vienna. Its materials and construction follow the organ building of Bach’s time, hence its nickname “Vienna Bach Organ”.

It is played at services and in organ concerts.

Sponsorship of St. Augustine

The Augustinian Church is known in Austria and far beyond for its solemn High Masses and, thanks to its centuries-old tradition, is regarded as a centre of church music. People come from all over the world to celebrate the High Masses with choir and orchestra.

This living tradition is at risk for financial reasons. The choir sings on a voluntary basis, but sheet music, fees for soloists and orchestra and the organisation cost a great deal of money. With your support the church music of St. Augustine will be preserved for the future.

Patron
